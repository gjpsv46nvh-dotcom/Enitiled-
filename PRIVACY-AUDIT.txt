FINALLY ENTITLED v11.4 — PRIVACY AUDIT

Audited browser-facing files for outbound data mechanisms.
- No form submission endpoint.
- No XMLHttpRequest, sendBeacon or WebSocket use.
- No cookies, localStorage or sessionStorage used for checker answers.
- No third-party analytics, advertising or social tracking scripts.
- The only scripted fetch is status.js requesting /data-status.json from the same site.
- Official government URLs are user-clicked links; checker answers are not appended to them.
- Content Security Policy restricts scripted network connections to the site's own origin.
- Referrer policy is set to no-referrer.

Hosting/network providers can still process ordinary technical request data needed to deliver and secure the website (for example IP address, device/browser information and request logs). This is separate from the household/financial answers entered in the checker.
